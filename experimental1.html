Experiment 1: Does Prompt Specificity Improve AI Image Output?

Tool Used: Google Nano Banana

Research Question: How does increasing prompt detail affect image quality, realism, and adherence to the request?

Prompt 1 - Basic

A futuristic city

The basic prompt generated a general futuristic city scene. The image contains modern buildings, but lacks strong stylistic direction or detailed environmental elements.

Prompt 2 - Moderately Detailed

A futuristic city at night with neon lights, flying cars, and tall glass skyscrapers

Adding descriptive elements like time of day and objects (flying cars, neon lights) produced a more dynamic scene. The image shows clearer structure and improved prompt adherence.

Prompt 3 - Highly Specific

A cyberpunk futuristic city at night, neon pink and blue lighting, flying cars in the sky, holographic advertisements, rainy streets reflecting light, ultra-detailed, cinematic lighting, 4K, wide angle

The highly specific prompt produced the most visually complex result. Lighting, atmosphere, and environmental details were significantly more refined. The output closely matched the requested cyberpunk aesthetic.

Conclusion

This experiment demonstrates that increasing prompt specificity significantly improves visual richness and stylistic accuracy. While basic prompts generate functional outputs, detailed prompts produce stronger composition, mood, and adherence to the intended style.
